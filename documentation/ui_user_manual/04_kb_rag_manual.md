# User Manual: KB RAG (Knowledge Base)

Chat with your documents (PDFs, policies, guidelines).

## Prerequisites
*   Documents must be in the `docs/` folder.
*   **Rebuild KB**: If the sidebar says "No Knowledge Base found", click **"Rebuild KB Index"** in the sidebar.

## How to Use
1.  **Ask a Question**: Type a question about your policies.
    *   *Example*: "What is the threshold for reporting a suspicious transaction?"
    *   *Example*: "Summarize the AML compliance requirements."
2.  **View Answer**: The AI answers based *only* on the provided documents.
3.  **Verify Sources**: Expand the "Context" or "Sources" section to see exactly which document paragraphs were used.

## Tips
*   **No Answer?**: If the AI says "I don't know", the information might not be in your documents.
*   **Refresh**: Use the "Generate Sample Questions" button to get ideas of what to ask.
