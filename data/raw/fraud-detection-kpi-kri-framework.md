# Fraud Detection KPI & KRI Framework

## 1. Key Performance Indicators (KPI)

| KPI | Definition | Formula | Target | Owner | Reporting Frequency |
|------|-----------|---------|--------|-------|--------------------|
| Fraud Detection Rate | % of fraud detected before loss | Detected Fraud / Total Fraud | >80% | Fraud Ops | Monthly |
| False Positive Rate | % of flagged cases not fraud | False Alerts / Total Alerts | <15% | Analytics | Monthly |
| Case Turnaround Time | Avg. investigation duration | Total Hours / Cases | <48 hrs | Fraud Ops | Weekly |
| Recovery Rate | % of funds recovered | Recovered / Total Loss | >40% | Recovery Team | Monthly |

---

## 2. Key Risk Indicators (KRI)

| KRI | Early Warning Signal | Threshold | Escalation Trigger |
|------|---------------------|------------|-------------------|
| Fraud Loss Ratio | Fraud Loss / Revenue | >0.5% | CRO escalation |
| Alert Volume Spike | % increase WoW | >30% | Root cause analysis |
| Model Drift | Population Stability Index | >0.25 | Model review |

---
