## 2. Risk Scoring Model

### Inherent Risk Score
Inherent Risk = Impact Score × Likelihood Score

| Score | Definition |
|-------|------------|
| 1 | Low |
| 2 | Medium |
| 3 | High |

Risk Rating Bands:
- 1–3: Low
- 4–6: Medium
- 7–9: High
